h1. Election

p(meta). 24 April 2011

So it has been a while since I’ve posted anything, but as some of you may know, I have been busy with getting ready to stand for the local elections in May. I’m standing for the Green Party in Odd Down, here in Bath. It has been a busy few weeks filling-in forms and sorting-out leaflets, but it’s only a few weeks until the election now and there’s little admin left to do, just leafleting, canvassing, and other actual ‘politics’.

So I will be a bit quiet until the election is over as it’s taking so much time. If you are a voter you can see our policies on our Web site here.

After looking at our policies, if you have any questions please either email me or leave a question on this page.
