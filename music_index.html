Dom Tristram - On Music

I have written and played music for some time. I try to do something new with every song - it might not be the best way to get better at what I already play, but it makes it more interesting to make.

I currently have two musical projects you can listen to:

  • The Goodness Guild - a fusion of folk, rock and electronica with lyrics
  • Radstock - electronica, cyberpunk and ambient instrumentals

I hope you'll give them a listen! I'm writing and recording more as time allows - if you 'follow' my artists on Spotify etc you'll be notified of new stuff.
